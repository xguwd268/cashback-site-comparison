## When does cashback become payable?

Cashback usually passes through several stages:

1. **Pending:** the platform has recorded the purchase, but the retailer has not finalized the commission.
2. **Confirmed:** the merchant has approved the transaction and the reward is available for withdrawal.
3. **Withdrawn:** the member requests payment through an available method.

ShopBack’s support documentation states that US members can withdraw through PayPal or ACH transfer. The help center also explains that only confirmed cashback can be withdrawn.

Confirmation time depends on the merchant. Retail purchases can remain pending for several weeks because the store needs time to process returns, cancellations, and order changes. Travel transactions may not confirm until after the stay, rental, or trip is completed.

One ShopBack guide lists a US$5 withdrawal threshold, while a separate later help article lists a US$10 minimum. Because these official pages conflict, the safer conclusion is that the minimum may depend on the current account or withdrawal configuration. Check the withdrawal screen in your own ShopBack account before relying on either figure.

That is also why a cashback balance should not be treated like an instant discount. You may see the expected reward in your activity shortly after ordering, but the money can remain unavailable until the merchant approves the sale.

## Which purchases make the most sense?

Cashback is most useful in categories where purchases are already planned and the order value is large enough for the reward to matter.

### Travel

Hotels, car rentals, flights, and activities can produce a larger dollar reward because the transaction value is higher. However, travel cashback can take longer to confirm. A booking may remain pending until after the stay or trip, and cancellation rules can affect eligibility.

### Electronics

Electronics purchases often have modest standard rates, but the dollar value can still make a small percentage worthwhile. Compare the final product price first. A higher cashback rate does not compensate for buying the same item at a higher base price.

### Fashion and beauty

These categories often have frequent promotions and seasonal sales. Check whether the offer applies to all products or only selected brands. Marketplace items, clearance products, and gift cards may be excluded.

### Everyday shopping

Food delivery, household goods, personal care, and recurring purchases can build a balance gradually. The individual rewards may be small, but the process becomes more useful when you consistently begin purchases through the portal.

### Gift cards

Gift card offers can have separate terms and may not earn cashback in the same way as ordinary products. Read the specific offer carefully before buying. A gift card transaction can also have different refund and cancellation rules from a normal retail order.
